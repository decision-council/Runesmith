    How it works

    Four motions, one ledger.

    Everything Runesmith does is written to a hash-chained ledger, so the history cannot be quietly rewritten. What it has not measured, it calls unknown.

    ENVIRONMENT.json

    It maps your world

    Every object in the folder: Python and Node projects, websites, document collections, plain folders, and projects nested inside folders. Each gets proposed objectives in bands (bad, minimal, optimal, world-class) and a build ladder with its next rung.

    SELF_MAP.json

    It maps itself

    A small, fixed kernel keeps budgets, judges and the ledger. The organs, the policies that do the work, are the only part that changes. Its capabilities on your work are measured, never assumed.

    propose, never impose

    It works under guard

    Repairs happen on private copies. A judge that runs tests the repair never saw must accept a fix before you see it. Applying is your click, checked against conflicts, backed up, and undoable.

    Kaizen, with a trial

    It improves only with evidence

    When one failure keeps recurring, Runesmith asks a model to rewrite the organ responsible. The candidate is tested on work its author never saw. Even then it is only frozen. It becomes active by winning a live trial on your work, and rolling back is one click.

    Runesmith Studio

    A control room for any shape and size.

    One command, or a double-click, opens the Studio in your browser. It runs on your computer only, reached through a private link. Walk through every screen in any of the three folders.

    Folder

    Comment on anything

    Press C and click an object, a rung, a fix, a model, or Runesmith itself. Your open notes travel with the work they are about, clearly labelled as your guidance.

    Undo everything

    Fixes apply only to unchanged files, keep your line endings and a backup, and undo byte for byte. Drafts never overwrite a file without asking you twice.

    Built for old laptops

    Standard-library Python, no build step, no framework, no fonts to download. Jobs run one at a time, so a modest machine never juggles two heavy things.

    Evidence

    Receipts, not claims.

    Runesmith is built as a falsifiable research programme. A capability counts only when a preregistered experiment, sealed before any outcome is read, measures it. An independent recomputation must then agree. Results that did not hold are published with the same weight.

    For one person or a whole company

    Trust that can be audited.

    Local by default

    The Studio listens on 127.0.0.1 only. It opens through a one-time link, refuses other hosts and refuses changes that come from other sites.

    Keys stay yours

    Keys are saved in the folder’s own home, outside your project files. They go only to their provider, and never back to the screen.

    Excluded means never read

    Mark a folder such as payroll or patient records as excluded, and Runesmith never opens it, copies it or sends it to a model.

    Propose, never impose

    Nothing is written without your click. Every write is checked for conflicts, backed up and undoable.

    A ledger you can verify

    Every decision is hash-chained to the one before. One click in Activity verifies the whole history, and a snapshot exports it for review.

    Guarded self-improvement

    Organs run confined, candidates are tested on held-out work, and only a won trial or your explicit choice switches one on. Improvements others share are checked, frozen and put on trial the same way.

    Get Runesmith

    Double-click and name what you will create.

    You need Python 3.11 or newer. To measure and repair Python code, also install pytest. Nothing else.

    Windows

    Double-click Runesmith.cmd. Drop a folder on it to work in that folder.

    macOS

    Double-click Runesmith.command. The first time, macOS may ask you to allow it in Privacy & Security.

    Linux

    Run ./runesmith.sh, or ./runesmith.sh ~/projects/my-idea for a specific folder.

    or from a terminal, in any folder
    # open the Studio for this folder
python -m runesmith

# or for another folder, on a chosen port
python -m runesmith up ~/work/northwind-freight --port 7400

    Questions

    Plainly answered.

    Do I need a GPU or a paid subscription?

    No. Without a model, Runesmith maps and watches. A small model on your own computer is enough to repair code, and a chat window you already use can serve as the Improver or the Planner.

    Will it change my files?

    Only when you click Apply on a fix or Write on a draft. It runs your tests on throwaway copies. Every write is checked against later changes, backed up and undoable, and folders you exclude are never read.

    Can a company use it on a large repository?

    Yes. Projects nested inside plain folders, such as services/billing, are found as objects of their own, and each suite runs on a throwaway copy, one job at a time. Folders such as payroll can be excluded, every fix waits for review, and the hash-chained ledger exports as a snapshot for audits. It is a research release, so keep your usual reviews.

    Does it only work on code?

    No. Websites get their own ladder (pages, styles, phone-ready, titles, working links), document collections are checked for an index and for internal links that resolve, and plain folders are mapped too. The link doctor proposes fixes for broken links without any model.

    What exactly does “improves itself” mean?

    Runesmith rewrites its own repair organ, the policy that decides what to read and how to ask the model. A rewrite is written by a model, tested on held-out work, frozen, and switched on only after it wins a live trial. In the sealed SR7 study, one such rewrite repaired 57 of 162 unseen tasks against its predecessor’s 35. That is one bounded result, and it has not been replicated yet.

    Can a weak model do real work in the suit?

    It can repair real code: a 2.6B free model did in exploratory runs. But the SR6-W study found no evidence that the suit made that model better than a strong simple scaffold: 5 against 12 of 68. We report that as clearly as the pass.

    Where are my keys?

    In .runesmith/secrets.json inside the folder you work in. That folder ignores itself for version control. Keys are sent only to their own provider and are never shown again.

    Is it ready for production?

    It is version 0.1, a research release. The kernel is small and tested, and every claim is tied to a receipt. Use it where a proposal-only assistant with undo is welcome, and keep your usual reviews.
